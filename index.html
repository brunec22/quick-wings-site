A colorful one-touch arcade game by Alen Brunec / AB DIGITECH.

How to play

Guide a little winged apple up through endless corridors. Touch and drag anywhere to move; avoid the walls, spinning saws, spike stars and sliding gates while the screen keeps scrolling faster. Collect stars to raise your score. Your best score is stored on your device. Use the speaker button to toggle sound.

Need help?

Contact support@abdigitech.net. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

No account is required to play. The game shows Google AdMob advertising between attempts and privacy choices where required. See the privacy policy for advertising data and consent details.
