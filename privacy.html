Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026. Quick Wings Classic is developed by Alen Brunec / AB DIGITECH, Slovenia.

Advertising and Google services

Quick Wings Classic uses Google AdMob to display advertising between game attempts. Google and its advertising partners may process data such as IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers (where permitted), ad interactions and usage information, and diagnostic data including crash and performance information. These services use data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and improve their services. Personalized ads depend on applicable consent and device permissions. Even non-personalized or limited ads may involve data processing.

The game does not require an account. The developer does not operate an account database or receive your advertising identifier directly from the game. Google processes advertising data according to its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Advertising data may be processed outside your country under the safeguards described in Google's policies; retention depends on the applicable provider's practices and legal obligations.

Consent and privacy choices

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a privacy message or consent choice is required. Where applicable, a consent message appears before advertising is initialized. Use Privacy choices on the menu or game-over screen, when available, to review or change your advertising choices. You can refuse or withdraw consent without losing access to gameplay; the ads available to you may change. If the service cannot establish that ads may be requested, the game continues without requesting ads.

On iPhone and iPad, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission after the advertising consent flow. You can decline and continue playing. You can change this permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. App Tracking Transparency and regional advertising consent are separate choices; allowing one does not override a refusal of the other. Device advertising settings are also available on Android.

On-device storage

Your best score and sound preference are saved locally on your device and are not sent to the developer. UMP and the operating system may also retain your privacy choices. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to device backup and restore settings. Some operating-system privacy preferences may remain under the operating system's control.

Children and advertising content

The game has no accounts or in-app messaging and is not submitted to Apple's Kids Category. The developer does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Advertising is supplied by Google subject to applicable consent, device settings and configured content restrictions. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with this app, please contact us. Store age ratings describe content suitability and are not a guarantee that third-party advertising involves no data processing.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Use the in-app privacy choices and device controls for advertising preferences. For data held by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and request procedures. Contact the developer for support correspondence or questions about this policy.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request and retained as needed to handle the conversation and legal obligations. Do not send passwords or payment details. This is separate from in-game advertising. This page contains no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts. Hosting providers may process technical request logs under their own policies.
